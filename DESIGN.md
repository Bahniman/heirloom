# Heirloom design

This site follows the Riso Poster system in `C:\Users\bahni\Downloads\Exports\Design Systems\Riso Poster\DESIGN.md`.

Use cream paper, ink outlines, blue and pink spot inks, and yellow only for sticker-like emphasis or active states. Use Bricolage Grotesque for display, Newsreader italic for the human voice, and Space Mono for labels and data. Use hard offset shadows and small-radius paper cards; tilt object-like decoration only. Keep long-form copy and all controls straight. Form and sandbox controls stay quiet, legible, keyboard accessible, and at least 44px tall. Support explicit light and dark themes consistently.

Keep the page reading-first, but make the opening feel like Heirloom's subject: a compact genealogy of fictional memory records. A yellow role-scope slip labels who may see a record, the paper record names its sample source, and a linked correction is explicitly conditional on a sandbox simulation. This record stack is static illustration; the role-query sandbox remains the only interactive stateful example near the opening. It uses a fixed fictional corpus and does not connect to an assistant or identity provider.

Use a bounded entrance for the paper stack, then direct hover feedback on the record objects. Keep the relation label readable, every control straight and at least 44px tall, and the section transitions reduced-motion safe. Use halftones only in a margin behind the stack, away from the records and their labels. Long-form argument and sources stay calm on paper; do not use glass, blur, gradients, or ornamental technical screens.
