# Heirloom design

This site follows the Riso Poster system in `C:\Users\bahni\Downloads\Exports\Design Systems\Riso Poster\DESIGN.md`.

Use cream paper, ink outlines, blue and pink spot inks, and yellow only for sticker-like emphasis or active states. Use Bricolage Grotesque for display, Newsreader italic for the human voice, and Space Mono for labels and data. Use hard offset shadows and small-radius paper cards; tilt object-like decoration only. Keep long-form copy and all controls straight. Form and sandbox controls stay quiet, legible, keyboard accessible, and at least 44px tall. Support explicit light and dark themes consistently.

Keep the page reading-first, but bring the role-query sandbox close to the opening. Use one strong poster headline and one blue demonstration stage; let the detailed argument and sources remain calm on paper. State clearly that the sandbox is illustrative and uses local sample data. Do not use glass, blur, gradients, or ornamental technical screens.
